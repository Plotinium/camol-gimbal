# Camol Gimbal

**Turn, flip and reshape your camos.**

Camol disguises any block as another, but small blocks come out facing whatever way they like. The
Gimbal puts you in charge: stand a camo trapdoor upright, lay a log on any side, open it, move it to
the top half, change anything its block can do.

## Features

- A radial menu with a 3D preview of every value.
- Copy a camo and paste it on one block, or on up to 64 at once: connected blocks, a face, a row or a
  column.
- Paste in the copied direction, turned with you, or following the face you click.
- A preview of the result before you click.
- See-through camos and an on-screen label while you hold the Gimbal.
- Camo Blocks follow the face you click (can be turned off).
- Camos drop as items when their block is destroyed by something other than a player.

Pasting a new camo costs what Camol costs: nothing is created for free.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge, and [Camol](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/camol). Install
on both client and server.

Camol Gimbal is an unofficial addon, not made by Camol's author. The radial menu is adapted from
Create's wrench menu (MIT license). No Create files are included and Create is not required.
